The world of Clarein only goes back a thousand years, due to the scourge that appeared in the Cleanse where the Weave purged Clarein and the surrounding planes of the scourge, and introduced [[Imbuements]] to the survivors. It was after this event where the races started fracturing, where all it took for a new cataclysm to be born was for a random farmer to stumble upon a powerful imbuement, relationships were more important than ever

After much deliberation, the party decided to head to the Gnome city of Tinkerholm, an arcane city of wonder and inventions. However upon arriving they found the way into the city barred, and were denied access by the cynical and sarcastic machine guard that refused them entry, eventually resorting to a bombardment to get them to leave. This killed one of the party, and gravely wounded another, so they sought refuge in a nearby town called Kursalbazaar, a seemingly normal town. Unfortunately they were not allowed to set up camp outside someones house, resulting in a small skirmish between the party and the local law enforcement (it took you guys one session). After seeing off the locals, the party took a room in the local inn, and went to sleep, before being rudely awakened by the dogged sheriff, who placed a hex on the party, which meant that if he didn't remove it by the time two days had passed, they would start feeling pain and be tormented until their death not too long after.
